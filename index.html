Why We Exist

The BAWO Foundation was created by Dr. Inara Omuso, MD, FACOG, and inspired by Dr. Aye Wilson Omuso, PhD. We are dedicated to providing health care services to under-resourced, marginalized, and vulnerable patient populations in the United States and sub-Saharan Africa.

Our mission is rooted in the legacy of Madam Bessie (Bawo) Wilson Omuso, a matriarch defined by generosity, love, and community commitment.

Our Core Pillars

We improve health and well-being through four key strategic areas.

Education

Specialized clinical training for safer deliveries and newborn care.

Research

Addressing health disparities in maternal and neonatal outcomes.

Advocacy

Championing policy for pregnant women and newborns.

Empowerment

Supporting mothers and families for better health outcomes.
